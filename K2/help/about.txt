
 Commodore 64 for Wildbits/K2

 C64MEGA65 by MJoergen & sy2002
 K2 port powered by MiSTer2MEGA65

 Quickstart:

 * Create a /c64 folder on your SD card &
   place your D64, CRT and PRG files there
 * You can work with long file names and
   with arbitrary sub-folders
 * Both SD card slots are supported. Back
   slot takes precedence over bottom slot
 * Copy the c64k2 config file to your
   /c64 folder so that your menu settings
   are being saved

 This board has no expansion port and no
 IEC connector, so hardware cartridges and
 real 1541/SD2IEC drives are unavailable.
 Simulated drives and CRT/PRG files from
 the SD card work as usual.

 Upstream docs: https://c64.mega65.org
