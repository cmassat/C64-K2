
 Commodore 64 for Wildbits/K2

 C64MEGA65 by MJoergen & sy2002
 K2 port powered by MiSTer2MEGA65

 While the C64 is running: hold C= and
 press RESTORE to mount drives and to
 configure the core.

 RESTORE on its own is the C64's own
 RESTORE key, so RUN/STOP + RESTORE still
 performs the usual warm reset.

 Both SD card slots work: The card in the
 back has higher precedence than the
 card at the bottom of the case.

 While you are in the file browser:
   F1: Switch to internal SD card
   F3: Switch to external SD card

 Output is HDMI only on this board.

 Press Space to continue.
