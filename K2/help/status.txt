
 Commodore 64 for Wildbits/K2

 SID:

 * For older productions choose 6581 and
   for newer productions choose 8580.
 * Stick to Mono SID unless you know what
   you are doing.

 Video:

 The K2 has no analog VGA output. The VGA
 and retro 15 kHz menu entries have no
 effect on this board.

 Writing to disk images:

 Wait until the drive led is done turning
 from green to yellow back and forth and
 is off again before unmount, reset or OFF.

 Board status:

 STAT_LED0 shows DDR3 calibration,
 STAT_LED1 shows reset. The built-in LCD
 identifies the loaded core.
