
 Commodore 64 for Wildbits/K2

 When browsing the menu:

 RESTORE:            Open/close menu
 Run/Stop:           Leave sub-menu
 Settings are saved when closing the menu

 When browsing for D64, CRT and PRG:

 Cursor up/down:     File up/down
 Cursor left/right:  Page up/down
 Run/Stop:           Cancel browsing
 F1:                 Bottom SD card
 F3:                 Back SD card
 Enter:              Mount drive
                     Load CRT or PRG
 Space:              Unmount drive

 The K2 has four independent cursor keys;
 no shifting is needed to move up or left.

 System reset:

 Use the menu entry to reset the C64 core.
 A reset also restarts cartridges.

 Crsr left: Prev  Crsr right: Next (2 of 3)
 Press Space to close the help screen.
